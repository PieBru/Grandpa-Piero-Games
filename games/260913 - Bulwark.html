    Field manual

    Six steps between you and a held line.

    1. Read the road. Raiders walk the dirt track from the west cave to your east gate. Only grass is buildable.
    2. Spend early. A Dart Post on a corner covers two legs of the path — corners beat straightaways.
    3. Mix your arsenal. Each repeat tower of one type costs 12% more than the last, but running all three types at once pays a bonus on every kill's coin and score. Pure spam stops scaling.
    4. Start waves for bonus coin. Every cleared wave pays out; chained waves compound your arsenal.
    5. Rebuild cheap. Sell a misplaced tower for 70% back. Right-click cancels a placement in progress.
    6. Save before you push your luck. Every ten waves the Warlord pack grows by one — bank a save file before a milestone wave.
    7. Mind the clock. In Timed Sprint the run ends the instant the wall clock hits 0:00 and your score locks in. In Sudden Death the clock expiring locks your arsenal and auto-chains waves at 4× until your last life is spent — build something that can hold itself.
